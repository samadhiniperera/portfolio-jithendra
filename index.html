02 — Experience

Engineering Background

Industrial Internship

Vega Innovations (Pvt) Ltd

2023 · Sri Lanka

Completed an industrial internship at Vega Innovations, a leading motorcycle manufacturer in Sri Lanka, contributing to both design and simulation tasks.

  • Designed and developed a reusable welding jig for the Vega Street Bike chassis using SolidWorks — ensuring dimensional accuracy and fabrication repeatability
  • Designed a rear paddock stand with full FEA validation in SolidWorks Simulation, physically fabricated and tested on-bike
  • Designed a quick jack mechanism for the Falcon E2 race car, optimised for pit-stop speed and packaging constraints
  • Conducted MATLAB/Simulink suspension dynamics analysis — modelling bump excitation response and investigating critical damping parameters for ride comfort optimisation
  • Redesigned the steering system of the Falcon E2 with improved Ackermann geometry and reduced bump steer
