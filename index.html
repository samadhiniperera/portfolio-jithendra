01 — About

Who I Am

I am a Mechanical Engineering graduate from the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka, having completed my B.Sc. Engineering (Hons.) with First Class Honours and a GPA of 3.73/4.00. I am currently awaiting my formal graduation.

My interests lie in Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), Fluid Mechanics, Computational Mechanics, Mechanical Design, Finite Element Analysis (FEA), and Aerospace Engineering. My work combines advanced numerical simulation with practical engineering, ranging from CFD-driven hydrodynamic research and structural analysis to the design, fabrication, and testing of engineering systems.

My final-year research focused on the Design and Development of a laboratory-scale hydrodynamic tunnel for experimental fluid dynamics, where I performed transient CFD simulations using ANSYS Fluent, optimized flow behaviour, and validated computational results through experimental testing. In addition, I gained industrial experience through the design and fabrication of motorcycle components, welding jigs, steering systems, and other mechanical systems, strengthening my understanding of engineering design, manufacturing, and product development.

Through my academic training at the University of Moratuwa—one of South Asia's leading engineering institutions—I have developed a strong foundation in engineering analysis, numerical modelling, and research-driven problem solving. I am passionate about applying computational and experimental methods to address complex challenges in fluid mechanics, structural mechanics, and multidisciplinary engineering.

I am currently seeking fully funded MSc or PhD opportunities, as well as Research Assistant positions, where I can contribute to cutting-edge research while further developing my expertise in computational engineering, fluid dynamics, and marine, offshore, or aerospace systems.

Design and Development of a Hydrodynamic Tunnel for Experimental Fluid Dynamics

Sri Lanka's first purpose-built hydrodynamic tunnel — a vertical, closed-loop facility designed, simulated, and fabricated from scratch to reproduce controlled flow regimes (Re 0–8,000) around a cylinder for external flow research. Final Year Project

Hydrodynamic Tunnel Research

2024 – 2026 · University of Moratuwa

Designed and developed a small/medium-scale closed-loop hydrodynamic tunnel targeting Re 0–8,000, producing steady, uniform, low-turbulence flow for external flow experiments around a cylindrical body.

  • Developed a MATLAB head-loss model confirming system head (~0.9 m) is well within pump capacity (~9 m)
  • Performed ANSYS Fluent CFD of the wide-angle diffuser; introduced internal guide vanes reducing effective diffusion angle to 7° per channel to eliminate flow separation
  • FEA of glass fibre components led to addition of ribs and reinforcements across all fabricated structural parts
  • Integrated hydrogen bubble flow visualisation into the test section for qualitative flow pattern observation
