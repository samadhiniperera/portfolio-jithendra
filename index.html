Who I Am

I am a final year Mechanical Engineering undergraduate at the University of Moratuwa, one of Sri Lanka's most prestigious engineering institutions. My passion lies at the intersection of design, simulation, and physical fabrication.

My academic journey spans CAD design, structural and fluid simulations, mechatronics, and manufacturing. I have delivered hands-on projects ranging from structural welding jigs and motorcycle components to MATLAB-based suspension dynamics and final-year hydrodynamic tunnel research.
