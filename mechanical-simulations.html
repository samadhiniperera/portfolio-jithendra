Front Suspension Analysis
Vega Street Bike — MATLAB

MATLABSimulinkDynamicsSuspensionInternship

During my internship at Vega Innovations, I performed a dynamic analysis of the Vega Street Bike front suspension using MATLAB/Simulink. A simplified dynamic model was developed to study the suspension response under bump excitation — evaluating chassis displacement and the effect of stiffness and damping parameters on ride behaviour.

  • MATLAB/Simulink dynamic suspension model developed
  • Bump excitation response simulation completed
  • Critical damping characteristics investigated
  • Suspension stiffness and damping parameter study
  • Simulation video included — use arrows to navigate
