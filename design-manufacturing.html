Reusable Welding Jig
Vega Street Bike Chassis

SolidWorksManufacturingJig DesignChassis

Designed and developed a reusable welding jig to support the accurate assembly of the Vega Street Bike chassis during fabrication. The primary objective was to ensure precise alignment and positioning of chassis members during welding, minimising dimensional errors and improving repeatability.

  • Precise chassis dimensional accuracy during assembly
  • Reduced fabrication time and improved repeatability
  • Reusable, modular jig design for multiple build cycles

Rear Paddock Stand
Vega Street Bike

SolidWorksFEAFabricationStructural Design

Designed and developed a rear paddock stand for the Vega Street Bike, enabling safe rear wheel elevation during maintenance. The process included concept development, full CAD modelling, and FEA stress validation in SolidWorks Simulation. The stand was physically fabricated and tested on the actual motorcycle.

  • Full SolidWorks 3D CAD model and assembly drawings
  • FEA stress and deformation analysis under load
  • Physical fabrication and real on-bike testing
  • Verified stability, usability and frame compatibility
