Front Suspension Analysis — Vega Street Bike

MATLABSimulinkDynamicsSuspension

Performed a dynamic analysis of the front suspension system of the Vega Street Bike using MATLAB/Simulink during an internship at Vega Innovations. A simplified dynamic model was developed to study suspension response under bump excitation. The simulation evaluated chassis displacement and analysed the effect of suspension stiffness and damping parameters on ride behaviour and vehicle stability.

  • MATLAB/Simulink dynamic model developed
  • Bump excitation response simulation
  • Critical damping characteristics investigated
