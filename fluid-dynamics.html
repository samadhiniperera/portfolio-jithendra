Final Year Project — Ongoing

My Final Year Project involves the full design, analysis, and development of a hydrodynamic tunnel facility. The project covers concept design and hydrodynamic modelling through to structural analysis and construction feasibility. CFD characterises flow behaviour and optimises the test section for uniform, low-turbulence flow.

Aerofoil CFD Simulations
ANSYS Fluent Analysis

CFDAerofoilANSYS FluentAerodynamics

Conducted CFD simulations analysing the aerodynamic performance of aerofoil sections. The study involved meshing aerofoil geometries, applying boundary conditions, and computing lift and drag coefficients at varying angles of attack. Results were validated against theoretical and published experimental data.

  • CFD simulation of aerofoil geometries in ANSYS Fluent
  • Lift and drag coefficient analysis vs angle of attack
  • Validation against theoretical and experimental data
