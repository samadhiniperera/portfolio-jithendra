Hydrodynamic Tunnel — Final Year Project (Ongoing)

FYPHydrodynamicsCFDTunnel DesignOngoing

This is my Final Year Project (FYP) involving the design, analysis and development of a hydrodynamic tunnel facility. The project covers the full engineering process from concept design and hydrodynamic modelling through to structural analysis and construction feasibility. CFD analysis is being conducted to characterise flow behaviour within the tunnel test section and optimise the design for uniform, low-turbulence flow.

  • Full hydrodynamic tunnel design and analysis
  • CFD flow characterisation of test section
  • Structural design and feasibility study
  • Project currently in progress — results to be added

Aerofoil CFD Simulations

CFDAerofoilANSYS FluentAerodynamics

Conducted computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulations to analyse the aerodynamic performance of aerofoil sections. The study involved meshing of aerofoil geometries, application of boundary conditions, and analysis of lift and drag coefficients at varying angles of attack. Results were compared against theoretical and published experimental data to validate the simulation methodology.

  • CFD simulation of aerofoil geometries
  • Lift and drag coefficient analysis vs angle of attack
  • Validation against theoretical and experimental results
