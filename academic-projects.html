Gearbox Design
Fine Material Washer — ME3201 Machine Design

Machine DesignGear CalculationsShaft DesignPET Recycling

A comprehensive gearbox design project for a fine material washer used in PET plastic recycling. Friction washer machines rely on high-speed water flow to clean PET flakes, but direct motor control causes inefficient cleaning, increased wear, and high initial costs. By introducing a gearbox, speed and torque are properly managed, improving cleaning performance and reducing operational and capital costs. The project covers the full mechanical design from power transmission layout through detailed gear and shaft calculations.

  • Full gearbox configuration selected to replace direct motor control
  • Detailed gear calculations — gear ratios, module, tooth geometry, and contact stress
  • Shaft design — input, lay, output, and idler shafts designed and stress-verified
  • Power transmission flow diagram and user requirements defined
  • Submitted: ME3201 Machine Design, Semester 5, University of Moratuwa

Walking Mechanism for Prosthetic Leg Fatigue Testing
ME4513 — Industrial Products & Machinery Development

Mechanism DesignProstheticsFatigue TestingGait Simulation

Prosthetic leg developers require reliable, repeatable mechanical testing systems to evaluate performance and durability before clinical trials. This project designed and developed a mechanically driven walking mechanism to simulate the human gait cycle for fatigue testing of a below-knee prosthetic leg. The mechanism reproduces the major gait phases — heel-strike, stance, toe-off, and swing — while applying realistic body weight equivalent loads in a controlled, safe, and repeatable manner. Emphasis was placed on simplicity, cost-effectiveness, and mechanical reliability throughout the design process.

  • Mechanism reproduces full gait cycle: heel-strike, stance, toe-off, and swing
  • Applies realistic body weight equivalent loads for fatigue life evaluation
  • Multiple concept designs screened against consumer need criteria
  • Final prototype design selected and detailed for fabrication
  • Design modifications proposed following prototype issue identification
  • Submitted: ME4513, December 2025, University of Moratuwa

Concept Design of an Ambulance Craft
ME4220 — Principles of Naval Architecture

Naval ArchitectureHull DesignHydrostaticsSolidWorks

Full concept design of a marine ambulance craft following a systematic naval architecture approach. The process began with a similar craft analysis to statistically derive principal dimensions — overall length, beam, draft, displacement, and block coefficient — from a database of existing marine ambulance vessels. Lines plans were generated and the 3D hull was developed and modelled in SolidWorks. Hydrostatic analysis and intact stability assessment were performed to verify the design meets seakeeping and safety requirements.

  • Similar craft statistical analysis — six parametric regression plots for dimension selection
  • Principal dimensions derived: Length, Beam, Draft, Displacement, Power, Block Coefficient
  • Lines plan generated and 3D hull developed in Rhinoceros and SolidWorks
  • Basic hydrostatics computed — displacement, waterplane area, metacentric height
  • Intact stability analysis performed and assessed against regulatory criteria
  • Submitted: ME4220, December 2025, University of Moratuwa

Reverse Engineering — Bicycle Air Pump
ME2171 — Manufacturing Engineering

Reverse EngineeringMaterial IdentificationSolidWorksManufacturing Analysis

A comprehensive reverse engineering study of a handheld bicycle air pump, dissecting and analysing constituent components to evaluate materials, manufacturing processes, and design limitations. Selected components including the pump barrel were systematically examined for material composition, manufacturing method, and structural performance. 3D CAD models were created in SolidWorks for all selected components, enabling detailed analysis of geometry, tolerances, and potential design modifications. The study identified opportunities for improvement through alternative materials and shape modifications to increase durability and load-bearing capacity.

  • Systematic disassembly and documentation of all pump components
  • Material identification — composition, hardness, and manufacturing suitability assessed
  • Casting and forming process analysis for each selected component
  • Non-metallic manufacturing process evaluation for applicable components
  • Full 3D SolidWorks models created for selected components
  • Design modification proposals for improved durability and load-bearing capacity
  • Submitted: ME2171 Manufacturing Engineering, Semester 4, University of Moratuwa
